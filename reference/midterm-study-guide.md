# Midterm Study Guide (instructor handout, Arabic 011)

Transcribed from the instructor's handout. The site's Midterm tab turns this into a checklist with links.

## Alif Baa

1. You must learn the complete Arabic alphabet, the letters, their connections, the short vowels, and hamza. Study pages through the Alif Baa book to review its vocabulary.
2. The Arabic numerals and their spellings.
3. The vocabulary from Alif Baa that we have been using daily.

## Al-Kitaab Review: Chapter 1

1. Vocabulary, and you should be able to use it in sentences and short phrases.
2. Conjugations for the verbs that we know already ("to go, to live, to study"), as well as others you may know from our lessons.
3. Maha's video. Understanding the content of this video orally and in written form. Be able to read or listen to a passage similar to this and be prepared to answer questions in full written Arabic sentences regarding the passage.
4. Gender. Nouns and adjectives always carry gender. Understand and be able to produce noun-adjective agreement based on whether the noun is masculine or feminine.
5. The definite article, "al-___". Please review it and pay attention to the usage of "al-___".
6. The nisba ending: indicating origin or affiliation, especially in reference to a place. For instance, Waalidee misree.
7. Asking questions: be able to select the correct question word. Be able to create a dialogue using these in a practical sense.
8. Possessive pronouns.
9. Case endings.
10. Nominal and verbal sentences (subject and predicate).
11. Idafa and noun-adjective phrases.

Make sure you know how to spell the vocabulary in Arabic.

## Also on the handout

- You need to know how to respond to greetings of: marHabaa, SabaaHil khayr, masaa'il khayr, assalaamu 'alaykum, ahlan wa sahlan.
- All letters connect to what follows EXCEPT FOR the six: alif, waaw, daal, dhaal, raa', zaay. These never connect to the letter after them.
